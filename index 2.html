About Carbon Coach

The problem we're solving, how the scoring works, and where we're headed.

Habit

People choose how they get around — commute, school run, errands — mindlessly. It's habit, not information.

Disconnected data

Air quality, pollen, and heat data exist. Carbon-impact data exists too. Neither is connected to that one daily choice.

Motivation gap

Pure carbon-tracking apps get abandoned fast. CO₂ savings alone aren't motivating enough, day after day.

Informational only

AQI apps tell you the number. They don't tell you what to actually do about it this morning.

Missing nudge

No tool combines "good for your health" and "good for the environment" into one nudge, at the exact moment the decision gets made.

Scoring model
score(mode) = carbon_saved − air_quality_penalty − heat_pollen_penalty − time_cost

Every morning, Carbon Coach weighs air quality, heat and pollen, time cost, and carbon savings across four transport modes — the same scores shown on your dashboard — and hands you a single answer: take X today, because Y. Biking usually scores high on carbon and time, but gets penalized hard the moment air quality turns poor or the heat index climbs into dangerous territory. The recommendation moves with the conditions, not a fixed rule.

01

02

It optimizes your route

Each morning, describe today's plans and the AI works out the best route, time, and mode of transport.

03

A connected maps feature guides you turn-by-turn to your destination in the recommended mode.

04

After you arrive, having tracked your speed and path, Carbon Coach adds your points to the monthly total.
